arXiv · Score: 61/100

Researchers propose the Memory Decision Layer (MDL), a zero-parameter controller for LLM agents that addresses the problem of trusting retrieved memories, especially when conflicting information exists. This approach significantly reduces hallucination rates in RAG systems under conflicting memories by about 56% and approaches zero hallucination in high-risk scenarios.
