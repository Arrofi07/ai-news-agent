The Exceedance Design Effect: Effective Sample Size for Thresholds under Clustering

arXiv · Score: 61/100

Machine learning systems often set thresholds (e.g., quantiles for conformal predictors or safety filters) assuming independent calibration data, but modern pipelines frequently use correlated data. This article introduces a new method to calculate the effective sample size for thresholds, which differs from methods for averages and varies depending on where the threshold is set.
