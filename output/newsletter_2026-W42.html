OccludeBench: Can VLMs Find What's Missing?

arXiv · Score: 61/100

Researchers introduced OccludeBench, a benchmark to evaluate how well Vision-Language Models (VLMs) can reason about fully occluded objects, distinguishing between direct physical cues and broader scene context. VLMs showed a significant 20-point performance gap between these two case types, unlike humans, with the primary bottleneck identified in recovering relevant objects from their projections within the full image. An "inpaint-then-confront" intervention, where models first generate a hidden object hypothesis and then evaluate it, significantly reduced this performance gap for models with image-generation capabilities.

💡 This research provides AI and Data Engineers with a critical diagnostic tool (OccludeTrace) and a benchmark (OccludeBench) to identify and address specific failure modes in VLM visual reasoning, especially concerning occluded objects. Understanding these limitations and potential mitigation strategies like "inpaint-then-confront" is crucial for building more robust and reliable multimodal AI applications.

RATED: Reliability-Aware AI Agent Tool Retrieval via Execution Dynamics

arXiv · Score: 61/100

RATED introduces a novel framework for AI agent tool retrieval that combines robust semantic matching with dynamic reliability adaptation. It employs a dual-encoder for initial semantic matching and a lightweight contextual bandit module to re-rank candidates based on real-time execution feedback. This approach significantly improves retrieval accuracy and reduces fault selection, especially during API outages, as demonstrated on the new EcoAgent benchmark.

💡 For AI and Data Engineers, RATED provides a critical solution to enhance the reliability of AI agents by dynamically adapting to real-world tool failures. This reduces operational overhead and improves the robustness of production AI systems, making agent deployment more practical and resilient.
