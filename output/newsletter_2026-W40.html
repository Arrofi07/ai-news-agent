Highlight-Then-Summarize: Learning to Compress Evidence for Long-Context Understanding

arXiv · Score: 61/100

Researchers propose Highlight-Then-Summarize (H2S), a new paradigm for long-context understanding where LLMs first identify relevant evidence and then integrate it into a compact, question-conditioned summary before generating an answer. 💡 This approach offers a practical solution for AI and Data Engineers dealing with the challenges of long-context LLM applications, enabling more efficient and cost-effective processing of lengthy documents by reducing token usage while improving reasoning accuracy. ClearGS: Reliability-Aware Gaussian Splatting from Handheld Videos

arXiv · Score: 60/100

ClearGS improves 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) from challenging handheld video footage by introducing Reliability-aware View Allocation (RVA) to intelligently weight frames based on quality and utility, and Render-Guided In-Video Restoration (RIVR) to restore degraded video observations without clean references. 💡 For AI/Data engineers working with 3D reconstruction or computer vision, ClearGS offers a robust method to generate high-quality 3D assets from real-world, imperfect video data, reducing the need for pristine capture environments and enabling more practical applications of 3DGS.
